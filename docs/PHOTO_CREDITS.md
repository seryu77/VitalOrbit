## To confirm before submitting
The four images the team uploaded themselves (ISS cover, Blue Marble, Mars globe, Earthrise) came
without their NASA IDs. Find each on https://images.nasa.gov or https://science.nasa.gov and paste
its source page and ID into the rows above. If one turns out not to be from NASA, replace it with
a NASA image.

## Candidates rejected
- Medical and ultrasound scenes (several ISS images) show identifiable crew faces.
- s130e009953 and iss007e11800 have captions or text burned into the image.
- iss038e013587 has a mission patch visible.
- s124e007018 (JPM interior) shows a national flag, an agency label and a mission ID.
